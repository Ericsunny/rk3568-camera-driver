IMX415 /dev/video5, 1920x1080 NV12, no detect/no OSD; capture three buffers and do not QBUF until both encoders destroyed. First encode identical held frames via memcpy, then import their EXPBUF fds to MPP and encode. Input buffers stay immutable. Require 3 complete outputs per mode, clean exit, both fully decode, compare decoded per-frame hashes; mismatch is a finding, never accept by rate alone. This bounded test does not establish continuous reuse, event/RTSP, production OSD, performance, or safe default replacement. Timeout is a hard failure.
