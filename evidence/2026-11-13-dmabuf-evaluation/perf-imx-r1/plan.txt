Bounded fixed-frame microbenchmark: /dev/video5 1920x1080 NV12 at nominal 15fps, one DQBUF frame held immutably without QBUF until all MPP contexts destroyed. Encode identical frozen frame 60 times for each run; modes copy,dmabuf,dmabuf,copy,copy,dmabuf; all no OSD/no detection. Measure process CPU and wall time inside per-mode loop including output fwrite; 60 complete encoded frames required per run, full decode and per-frame comparison. Continuous capture, OSD, detection, RTSP, end-to-end latency and safe dynamic reuse are explicitly untested. No replacement based on this microbenchmark.
