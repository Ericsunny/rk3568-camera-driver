Bounded fixed-frame microbenchmark: /dev/video14 1280x960 NV12 at nominal 30fps; one captured frame held immutable without QBUF until all MPP contexts destroyed. Encode same frame 60 times per run in order copy,dmabuf,dmabuf,copy,copy,dmabuf; no OSD/no detection. Measure process CPU/wall in each loop including output fwrite; 60 complete packets required per mode; verify bitstreams and decode at vsync 0. Continuous capture, OSD, detection, RTSP, end-to-end latency and dynamic reuse are untested. No default replacement from this microbenchmark.
